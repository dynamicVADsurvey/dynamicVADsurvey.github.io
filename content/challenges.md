# Challenges and Future Directions

## Reliable semantic reasoning

Language models can make anomaly decisions more legible while also introducing hallucination and prompt sensitivity. Future work needs grounded explanations that can be traced to time and space.

## Comparable evaluation

The field needs explicit protocols for open-vocabulary queries, external knowledge, latency, and explanation quality. Reproducible benchmark metadata is as important as aggregate scores.

## Sustainable synthesis

Dynamic surveys must update quickly without destabilizing their taxonomy or rewriting conclusions automatically. Localized edits, confidence-aware abstention, and versioned human review offer a practical path.
