# Training-Free Methods

Training-free VAD composes pretrained components without task-specific parameter updates. Caption generation is one route, but the category also includes retrieval, event-aware reasoning, temporal aggregation, and agentic pipelines [2].

## Caption-based temporal reasoning

Methods such as the representative LAVAD record translate clips into text-like evidence and ask a language model to reason over events. This can produce interpretable outputs, though the reliability of descriptions and prompts remains critical.

## Retrieval and agents

Retrieval can ground decisions in examples or knowledge. Agentic systems can decompose observation, hypothesis generation, verification, and aggregation, but public survey claims still require human review.
