# Introduction

Video anomaly detection (VAD) aims to identify events that depart from expected activity in video. Language models change this setting by adding semantic knowledge, open-ended descriptions, and reasoning interfaces to conventional visual anomaly scores [1].

This first web release is a **representative sample edition**. It demonstrates the intended reading, exploration, and versioning system while the complete reviewed manuscript is prepared for import.

## Why language models?

Traditional VAD systems often learn regularity from visual data and flag deviations. Vision-language and large language models can additionally name events, connect them to context, retrieve relevant concepts, and explain why an observation may be unusual.

## Scope

The survey organizes work by supervision, training strategy, model role, and the type of anomaly understanding produced. It treats structured records as navigational evidence while reserving interpretive claims for human-reviewed prose.
